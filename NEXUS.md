# Nexus page draft - Click To Choose

**Short description:** Customization options apply only when you click them, not when you hover.

**Description:**

In Farm Together 2's character customization, merely moving the mouse over a hair style (or any option
tile) selects it, so you cannot sweep the mouse across the grid, or over to the character to rotate it,
without changing your look. Click To Choose makes options apply on click only. Hover still highlights.
Keyboard and gamepad navigation are unchanged. Other screens (shops, menus) are untouched.

**Requirements:** BepInEx 6.0.0-be.793 Unity IL2CPP (win-x64).

**Install:** extract over the game folder, or install with Vortex.

**Config:** `General.Enabled` (default true), `General.VerboseLogging` (default false).

**Known issues:** none yet. Screenshots/banner still to make.
